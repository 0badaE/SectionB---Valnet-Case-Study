title: Transferring GI Bill benefits to family members
source: DoD / VA public guidance (adapted)

last_reviewed: 2025-01-10

Eligible service members can transfer unused Post-9/11 GI Bill benefits to a spouse or children. To be eligible you must be on active duty or in the Selected Reserve, have at least 6 years of service, and agree to serve 4 more years.

The transfer must be requested while you are still serving, through the Department of Defense (milConnect). You cannot request a transfer after you have separated or retired. Once transferred, the family member applies to VA for the benefit.
