SkillBridge lets eligible service members gain civilian work experience through an internship or training with an industry partner during their last 180 days of service. Participants continue to receive their military pay and benefits.

You need your commander's approval and must have completed required transition steps first. Program availability varies by service branch and installation, so start the conversation with your transition office well before the 180-day window opens.
