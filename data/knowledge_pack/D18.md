Many states offer their own veterans benefits, such as property tax exemptions, tuition assistance, special license plates and hiring preferences. Eligibility and amounts differ widely from state to state and are set by each state.

For details, contact your state's Department of Veterans Affairs or county veterans service office. This article does not list state-by-state rules.
