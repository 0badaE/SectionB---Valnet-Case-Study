The Post-9/11 GI Bill provides up to 36 months of education benefits. You generally qualify with at least 90 days of aggregate active-duty service after September 10, 2001, or if you were discharged with a service-connected disability after 30 days. Your benefit level is 40% to 100% depending on how long you served.

When you use it at an approved school, the benefit can pay tuition and fees directly to the school, a monthly housing allowance based on the school's ZIP code, and a yearly books and supplies stipend. Benefit levels and rates are set by VA; confirm your own percentage by applying for a Certificate of Eligibility.
