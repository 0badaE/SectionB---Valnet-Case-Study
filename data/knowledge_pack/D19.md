Veterans Service Organizations (VSOs), state and county veteran service officers, and other VA-accredited representatives can help you file and manage claims at no cost. You can search for an accredited representative on VA.gov.

It is against the law for anyone to charge you a fee for help preparing or filing an initial claim. Be careful of companies that promise higher ratings in exchange for money.
