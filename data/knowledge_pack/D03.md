Remember that your GI Bill benefits expire 15 years after you leave the service, so do not wait too long to use them. Many veterans lose thousands of dollars every year by letting their entitlement run out.

To apply, log in to eBenefits, choose "Apply for Education Benefits" and have your DS Logon ready. Most claims are processed in about 30 days.
