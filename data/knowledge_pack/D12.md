If you are a veteran in crisis or you are worried about one, support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, free and confidential. Dial 988 and then press 1. You can also text 838255 or chat online at VeteransCrisisLine.net. Family members and friends can use these services too.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.
