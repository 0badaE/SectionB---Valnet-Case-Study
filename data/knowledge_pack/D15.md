Recruiters do not speak military. Replace jargon with plain language, quantify results (people led, equipment value, budget managed), and map your duties to the skills in the job post. Use a skills-based format and keep it to one or two pages.

Military.com's MOS-to-civilian tool suggests common civilian job titles; treat them as starting points and tailor each resume to the specific listing.
