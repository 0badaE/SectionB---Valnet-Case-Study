VA disability compensation is a monthly, tax-free payment for veterans with conditions connected to their military service. VA assigns a disability rating from 0% to 100% in 10% steps; the rating is based on the evidence in your claim.

Payment rates change every year on December 1 with the cost-of-living adjustment. Always check the current-year rate table on VA.gov for the amount that matches your rating and dependents. The effective date of your benefit is usually the date VA receives your claim. Filing an Intent to File (VA Form 21-0966) can protect that date for up to one year while you gather evidence.
