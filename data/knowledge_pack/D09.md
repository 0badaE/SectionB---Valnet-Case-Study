The PACT Act, signed in August 2022, expanded VA health care and disability benefits for veterans exposed to burn pits, Agent Orange and other toxins. It added many presumptive conditions: if you served in a covered location during a covered period and have a listed condition, VA presumes the condition is service connected.

Veterans who served in Iraq, Afghanistan and other listed Southwest Asia locations may have been exposed to burn pits. You can use the VA.gov screening tools to see whether you may be eligible, and you can apply for health care or file a claim at any time. VA makes the final eligibility decision.
