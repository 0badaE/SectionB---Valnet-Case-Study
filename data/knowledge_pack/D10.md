The VA home loan guaranty helps eligible veterans, service members and some surviving spouses buy, build or refinance a home. Most VA-backed loans do not require a down payment and do not require private mortgage insurance.

You start by requesting a Certificate of Eligibility (COE). Most borrowers pay a one-time VA funding fee, but veterans who receive VA disability compensation are exempt from the funding fee. VA does not make the loan itself: private lenders set interest rates and credit requirements, so compare offers from more than one lender.
