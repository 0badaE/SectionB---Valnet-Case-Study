Many retailers and restaurants offer military and veteran discounts, but programs, amounts and eligibility vary by company and by store, and they can change without notice. Most online retailers verify status through a third-party service such as ID.me or SheerID; in stores you may be asked for a military ID, VA ID card or discharge papers.

Always confirm the offer with the retailer before you buy. Discounts are never guaranteed.
