TAP is a required program for separating service members. It includes workshops on benefits, career planning and resume writing. Service members should begin transition planning as early as possible: at least 12 months before separation, or 24 months before retirement.

Your installation's transition office schedules TAP. Completing TAP is also how you build an Individual Transition Plan, which is a prerequisite for programs such as SkillBridge.
