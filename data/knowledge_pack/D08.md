Most veterans who served in the active military and were discharged under conditions other than dishonorable can apply for VA health care. VA places enrolled veterans into priority groups 1 through 8, which affect copays and wait for some services.

You can apply online at VA.gov, by phone, by mail, or in person at a VA medical center. Veterans who served in locations with certain toxic exposures may qualify for enrollment under expanded eligibility rules (see the PACT Act overview). Having a VA disability rating can place you in a higher priority group.
