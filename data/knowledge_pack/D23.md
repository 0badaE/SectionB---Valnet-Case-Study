The assistant only uses information from your profile (such as branch, service dates, disability rating, state and dependents) to personalize answers if you have turned on personalization. If personalization is off, the assistant answers using only what you type in the chat.

Do not enter Social Security numbers, VA file numbers, bank details or medical records in the chat. The assistant cannot access your VA account or look up claims. Chat logs are kept for 30 days and are scrubbed of personal identifiers before storage.
