The fastest way to track your VA claim is eBenefits. Sign in with your DS Logon Premium account, select "Track Claims" and review the stage your claim is in (claim received, initial review, evidence gathering, review of evidence, preparation for decision, pending decision approval, notification).

If your DS Logon is not working, you can call the eBenefits help line for a reset.
