title: Forever GI Bill: time limits on using your benefit
source: VA public guidance (adapted)
source_type: official
published: 2024-11-05
last_reviewed: 2025-02-15
audience: all
---

If your last discharge or release from active duty was on or after January 1, 2013, your Post-9/11 GI Bill benefits do not expire. There is no 15-year time limit for you.

If your last discharge or release was before January 1, 2013, your benefits generally expire 15 years after your last period of active duty of at least 90 consecutive days. Some exceptions apply, so veterans in this group should confirm their own end date with VA before making plans.
