DEA provides education and training benefits to eligible children and spouses of veterans who died or are permanently and totally disabled because of a service-connected condition. Eligible family members may receive up to 36 months of benefits.

DEA is separate from Post-9/11 GI Bill transfer. Eligibility is determined by VA based on the veteran's service-connected disability status; apply through VA.gov.
