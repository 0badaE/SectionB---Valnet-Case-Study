You can file a VA disability claim online at VA.gov, by mail, in person at a regional office, or with help from an accredited representative. To use VA.gov you sign in with a Login.gov or ID.me account.

To check the status of a claim or appeal, sign in to VA.gov and open your claim status page. You can also upload evidence there. If you do not have an account yet, create one first; the older eBenefits and DS Logon sign-in options are being retired for these tasks.
